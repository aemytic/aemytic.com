Amith K. B.

Born in Kochi, Kerala, on 19 September 2007, Amith K. B., known as Aemytic, is a Software Development student with interests across technology, literature, music, cinema, visual art, philosophy, and psychology.

His interest in Malayalam language and literature began early. He was awarded the Kunjunni Maash Vidyabyasa Puraskaram and participated in poetry and short-story writing competitions. Many of these works were created specifically for competitions and were never formally published, but writing remained an important part of his creative work.

Music became another part of his creative life. He participated in Malayalam poetry singing and light music at school and district-level competitions, including a performance of Nellikka by Murukan Kattakada. Through school activities and Nanma Balayarangu, he continued participating beyond the school environment and received district-level recognition in poetry singing and light music. He also received a Best Student Scholarship in recognition of his overall student performance.

His interest in storytelling later expanded into filmmaking. He has directed short-film projects including Dracula and The Gift, with The Gift remaining unreleased. These projects introduced him to directing, atmosphere, narrative, and visual storytelling.

Today, he is pursuing an Honours degree in Software Development while continuing to explore philosophy, psychology, literature, cinema, music, visual art, technology, and unconventional ideas. He is particularly interested in human nature, abstract thought, psychological cinema, experimental filmmaking, distinctive visual aesthetics, and perspectives that challenge familiar ways of seeing.

For Amith, code, cinema, literature, music, and art are different ways of exploring ideas and expressing them. Aemytic is the space where those interests come together: a personal place for the things he creates, discovers, and finds meaningful. He is still figuring out where all of it will lead. For now, the intention is simple: to keep learning, keep creating, and keep becoming someone he hasn't met yet.
